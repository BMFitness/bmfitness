Treadmill Care Guide

Hover over any card to view maintenance tips and protect your device.

Treadmill Status: 100%

1. Belt Lubrication

Every 1 to 3 months with specialized oil.

2. Power Protection

Unplug or use a surge protector strip.

3. Weight Capacity

Keep a 10-15 kg margin below max weight.

4. Proper Startup

Stand on side rails before starting.

5. Motor Rest

Rest the machine after 45 minutes of use.

6. Regular Cleaning

Wipe down and clean beneath the belt.

7. Running Shoes

Wear proper shock-absorbing shoes.

8. Fault Warnings

Stop immediately if strange sounds occur.
